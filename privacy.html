SubmitWise

Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026. SubmitWise is developed by Ganesh Kumar Pandey.

What the app stores

The paper details you enter (titles, journals, manuscript IDs, statuses, dates, notes), your email signature and, if you buy Pro, your licence email and unlock code. All of it stays on your phone in the app's private storage.

What the app sends

Nothing. Screenshots and photos you scan are read on your phone and never uploaded. SubmitWise has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no account. Unlock codes are checked on your phone, offline. We never receive your papers or anything else you enter.

Buying Pro

When you buy Pro directly, you send your payment by UPI and email us your transaction ID and an email address. We keep a record of the email address, date and transaction ID to issue and support your licence, and use it for nothing else. If the app is later sold through Google Play, those purchases are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Backups

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include SubmitWise data in your device backup, under Google's terms. You can also export a backup file yourself; where you save or share it is your choice.

Notifications and email

Notifications are used only for inquiry reminders and the weekly check-in, and can be turned off in Android settings. The "Open email" button hands a draft to an email app you choose; SubmitWise does not send email itself.

Deleting your data

Delete papers in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything stored on the phone. To have your purchase record deleted, email us.
