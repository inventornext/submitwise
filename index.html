Questions

Does SubmitWise log into Editorial Manager for me?

No. You still check the journal site, then update the status in SubmitWise with two taps. Logging in for you would mean storing your journal password, which is a risk we won't take. The weekly check-in makes keeping it current quick.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It will be. For now it's distributed directly, which keeps the price low. The app is signed by the developer and the download comes from this site's GitHub releases.

What if I change phones?

Enter the same email and unlock code again. Use Export backup in Pro to move your papers.

Can I get a refund?

If Pro doesn't work for you within 7 days of buying, email with your UTR and you'll get a full refund.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your papers stay on your phone. There is no account, no analytics and no server. Privacy policy.
